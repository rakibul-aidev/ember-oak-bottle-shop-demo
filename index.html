Who we are

We taste everything before you see it.

Ember & Oak started as one shelf of bottles a friend actually recommended, instead of whatever the distributor pushed that month. It's grown into a full shop, but the rule hasn't changed: if we haven't tasted it, it doesn't go on the shelf.

Everything below is real inventory, described honestly. No filler bottles, no "trust us." Come smell the difference.

"Buy less, drink better."

One thing to try

Pull the pour

Press and hold the button to fill the glass and reveal a recipe. Release early and it settles back down.

Oak & Ember Old Fashioned

  • 2 oz bourbon (ask us for this week's pick from the whiskey wall)
  • 1 sugar cube, 2 dashes aromatic bitters
  • Stir over ice, express an orange peel over the top

The live Pour Guide swaps this for whatever's actually on your shelf. Reduced motion visitors get the finished glass and recipe with no hold required.
